01 — About

From island grids
to the energy transition.

I spent over a decade at PT PLN Indonesia — building SCADA and distribution automation from the ground up, leading planning for isolated island systems across the Riau archipelago, and delivering projects measured in millions of euros saved and thousands of tonnes of CO₂ avoided.

In 2024 I earned a Swedish Institute Scholarship to pursue an MSc in Sustainable Energy Engineering at KTH in Stockholm, completed in 2026 with a weighted average of 4.45/5.0. My thesis — developed with Smålandshamnar AB and Oskarshamn Energi — sized a PV, battery storage, and shore-power system for Oskarshamn Harbour using MILP dispatch optimisation and genetic algorithms.

Today I work at the intersection of both worlds: deep operational knowledge of real grids, and state-of-the-art modelling of solar, storage, and hybrid energy systems.
