01 · About

From island grids
to the energy transition.

I am a power systems automation and OT integration engineer with 15+ years at PT PLN (Persero) Indonesia, bridging field-proven utility operations with control, protection, and grid-communications engineering. From building regional SCADA/ADMS and self-healing feeders from the ground up to leading generation and submarine-cable planning across isolated archipelagos, my work translates directly into millions of euros saved and thousands of tonnes of CO₂ avoided each year.

In 2024 I was awarded the Swedish Institute Scholarship for Global Professionals (SISGP) — granted to the top ~2% of applicants worldwide — to complete a full-time MSc in Sustainable Energy Engineering at KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm (graduated June 2026, weighted average 4.45 / 5.0). At KTH, I specialised in mathematical optimisation, grid-scale storage, and AI forecasting, culminating in an industry-partnered harbour electrification thesis ↓.

Since July 2026 I serve at PT PLN Head Office in Jakarta as Senior Engineer for T&D Technology Development, authoring minimum technical specifications (MTS) for utility-scale BESS and evaluating storage architectures under Indonesia's 100 GWp solar PV programme — while advising developers, EPCs, and investors as an independent energy-modelling consultant.

Flagship MSc Thesis · KTH × Industry Smålandshamnar AB · Oskarshamn Energi

Oskarshamn Harbour: Sustainable Energy System Sizing

Designed and optimised an integrated rooftop solar PV, battery energy storage (BESS), and onshore power supply (OPS) architecture for berthed vessels at a working Swedish port. Coupled a rolling-horizon Pyomo MILP dispatch engine with a DEAP genetic algorithm to map multi-objective Pareto trade-offs across NPV, CO₂ emissions, and port self-sufficiency — benchmarking LFP, sodium-ion, and vanadium-redox-flow chemistries using PNNL decomposed CAPEX models.
